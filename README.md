# Free Indeed

A free Bible reading app with the background and context you need.

"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed." John 8:36

- Full Bible: King James Version and Bible in Basic English (both public domain in the United States, where the app is hosted). No words added.
- A short note for every book: who wrote it, when, what the world was like, why it is in the Bible. Its "Where to start" line has a button for each suggested chapter or passage. Tap one and it opens just that passage, with a button to read the whole chapter if you want it. The "also read" and related-verse references open the same way.
- "In context" notes for verses that are often taken out of context.
- Reading plans, each with an About page and short daily readings: New to the Faith, The Gospel of John, The Gospel of Mark, Acts, Romans and Ephesians, Psalms, Proverbs, New Testament in a Year, and the Whole Bible in a Year.

Free for everyone, always. Share the link as it is. Not to be sold, changed or put under another name. See LICENSE.txt.

Official version: https://freeindeedbible.github.io/free-indeed/

- A map of Bible places: open the Map button while reading any chapter that names a place, or see The story, then Where it happened. Each place shows its name in the Bible and its name today.

- Today: opens on a home screen with a verse of the day (it changes every day; tap the verse to open its chapter with the verse highlighted), where you left off in your reading plan, your streak and shortcuts.
- Text size: the Aa button makes all text larger or smaller, up to a very large size.
- Highlights in five colours: tap a verse, pick a colour. You can also write a note.
- Related verses: tap a verse, then Related verses, to see other places in the Bible that connect with it. Only lists of verses, no words added. Cross references from OpenBible.info (openbible.info/labs/cross-references), under a Creative Commons Attribution licence (CC BY).
- Reading plans show a few "if you have time, also read" references for each day.
- Back button: after you leave a chapter, a "Back to where you were" bar returns you to your place.
- Bible text checked verse by verse (15 Nov 2026): stray margin notes removed from 41 KJV verses, psalm titles and Psalm 119 headings added, Psalm 76 numbering fixed in the Simple English version.
- Each reading plan has its own calm colour on its card and day screens, so it is easy to find.
- The maps now sit on real terrain (hills, valleys and sea depth) from Natural Earth, which is public domain, with accurate lake and river outlines. Everything drawn on top is still only from the Bible.
- A "New here? Start here" card sits at the top of Today until it has been opened twice. After that it lives in the Go to tiles. The six Go to tiles have soft colours.
- The Jerusalem map has a soft, clearer look. The map footnote is updated.
- Two new reading plans, Scripture only: Who is Jesus (14 readings) and The Cross and the Empty Tomb (13 readings).
- A Share Free Indeed button on the Start page opens the phone's share menu with the official link only.
- Lakes show in a proper blue in dark mode.
