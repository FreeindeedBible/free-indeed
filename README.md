# Free Indeed

A free Bible reading app with the background and context you need.

"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed." John 8:36

- Full Bible: King James Version and Bible in Basic English (both public domain). No words added.
- A short note for every book: who wrote it, when, what the world was like, why it is in the Bible.
- "In context" notes for verses that are often taken out of context.
- Reading plans: New to the Faith, The Gospel of John, Psalms and Proverbs.

Free for everyone, always. Share the link as it is. Not to be sold, changed or put under another name. See LICENSE.txt.

Official version: https://freeindeedbible.github.io/free-indeed/
